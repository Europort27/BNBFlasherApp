02 FIELD GUIDE

Flash instructions

WEB SERIAL ESP32 FAMILY
A

BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Connect and authorize your board

Use a data USB cable

Connect the ESP32 board directly to this computer. Charge-only cables will not expose a serial port.

Use Chrome or Edge

Web Serial requires a supported browser and a secure page. HTTPS and localhost are supported.

Close serial monitors

Only one application can own a serial port at a time. Close other terminals using the board before flashing.

B

FLASH A DEVICE

Choose the matching firmware

  1. 01

    Select a profile

    Master is for the ESP32-WROOM-32E. Wired scanner is the C5 connected to the master by UART. Wireless scanner is a C5 joining over ESP-NOW.

  2. 02

    Grant serial access

    On the Flasher page, choose Choose USB device and select the board in the browser prompt. The permitted device appears in the serial selector.

  3. 03

    Start flashing

    Choose the detected device profile and press Flash firmware. The browser identifies the chip before writing. If the chip does not match the profile, flashing stops.

  4. 04

    Wait for completion

    Keep the USB cable connected while the job console reports progress. The board resets when writing and verification finish.
